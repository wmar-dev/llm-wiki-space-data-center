# Meet Project Suncatcher, a research moonshot to scale machine learning compute in space

Source: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/research/google-project-suncatcher/
Publisher: Google (The Keyword)
Fetched: 2026-09-24

## Summary

Project Suncatcher is Google's research moonshot exploring how an interconnected
network of solar-powered satellites, equipped with Google Tensor Processing Unit (TPU)
AI chips, could scale machine learning compute using solar power in orbit.

## Timeline

Google plans to launch two prototype satellites in partnership with Planet by early
2027 to test the architecture in orbit.

## Hardware

Solar-powered satellites equipped with Google TPU AI chips. Radiation testing of
Google TPUs has been conducted as part of feasibility work.

## Research Output

Google has published a preprint paper describing satellite constellation design,
control, and communication for the proposed system (see companion technical blog:
raw/google-suncatcher-research-technical.md).
